Booking & Policy Terms

The fine print, kept short

Peak Season
Hajj period and the last 10 days of Ramadan carry a flat 25% surcharge, disclosed at the time of quote, not applied as a surprise at pickup.
Cancellation
Free cancellation up to 48 hours before scheduled pickup. Inside 48 hours, 50% of the fare applies. No-shows are charged in full. Confirm these windows against your TGA/Nusuk operator terms once the licensing pathway is finalized; Nusuk Masar's mandatory pre-booking rules may tighten this further for certain routes.
Currency
SAR is the operating currency. At the fixed peg of 1 USD = 3.75 SAR, the entry Signature Sedan transfer runs approximately $147. GBP, EUR, and PKR equivalents float and should be shown live on the website via a currency toggle rather than printed here.
Waiting Time
Complimentary wait covers 60 minutes for international arrivals, 30 minutes for domestic. Beyond that, standard hourly extension rates apply per tier.
Group Convoys
Hajj groups requiring multiple vehicles moving together are quoted as a convoy package with one lead coordinator; ask your account contact for a group rate.
