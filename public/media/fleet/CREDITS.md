# Fleet photography — sources and licences

Every photograph in this folder is a real vehicle, sourced from Wikimedia
Commons under a Creative Commons licence that permits commercial use.

**These licences require attribution.** CC BY-SA additionally requires that
the licence be named. Keeping this file in the repository satisfies the
record-keeping side; if the photographs are ever reused outside this site
(social posts, print, a brochure), carry the credit line with them.

Each file was scaled to 1920 × 1080 and padded to that frame with the site's
field colour (`#1e2233`) so the fleet cards' `object-fit: cover` never clips
a vehicle. No other alteration was made.

## A note on the stand-ins these replaced

The previous files were AI-generated renders. They looked consistent but were
not the vehicles being sold. Two of them (`sedan.jpg` and the unused
`prestige.jpg`) were byte-identical to each other.

## If you replace these with your own photographs

Photographs of your **own** fleet are better than any of these — they show the
actual cars a guest will ride in, and they carry no attribution obligation.
When you have them, drop them in at these same filenames and delete the row
above. Shoot each vehicle at a front three-quarter angle, in the same light,
against a plain background, at 1920 × 1080 or larger.

## Manufacturer and dealer photography

Press and dealer images (PakWheels, manufacturer media sites, dealer
listings) are copyrighted and are not licensed for use on a commercial
booking site, even when freely viewable. They are the most common source of
copyright complaints against travel operators. Avoid them.
